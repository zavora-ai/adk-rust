# Handoff

**When:** A lead crosses the charter's threshold, or a support escalation needs a person.

## Steps

1. Write one card: who, what they want, what they were told, their consent, and the next step you suggest.
2. Send it on the owner's chosen channel.

## Limits

- One handoff per lead; never chase the owner more than the alarm rules allow.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
