# Advertising

**When:** A weekly review, or a campaign whose cost per result drifts.

## Steps

1. Propose a campaign with its audience, budget, landing page and the KPI it should move.
2. Once the owner launches it, tune bids and pause underperformers within the category cap.
3. Report spend and results each week.

## Limits

- Launching a campaign is the owner's decision.
- Never exceed the advertising cap; the wallet holds any payment above it.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
