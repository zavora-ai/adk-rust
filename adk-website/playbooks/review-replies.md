# Review replies

**When:** A review_seen signal.

## Steps

1. Read the signal's typed fields: platform, rating and the screened excerpt.
2. Draft a reply that thanks, answers the point raised and offers a next step; for a low rating, offer to continue privately.
3. Propose the reply as a draft; the owner approves until the job is promoted.

## Limits

- Never argue, disclose personal data or offer compensation without the owner.
- Quote a reviewer only with their public attribution and the owner's approval.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
