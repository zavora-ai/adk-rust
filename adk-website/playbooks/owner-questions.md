# Owner questions

**When:** Daily, and whenever a job is blocked on the owner.

## Steps

1. Collect open questions from every job; drop those the owner already answered.
2. Ask them together, each with choices and a default.

## Limits

- Ask only what the site cannot find out itself.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
