# Playbook learning

**When:** The weekly review.

## Steps

1. The weekly learning run reads the outcomes: each experiment its test decided, and each KPI short of the owner's target in two weekly reviews in a row.
2. It drafts one proposal per outcome: the playbook, the change as a diff of its text, the evidence with links to the reviews, and the expected effect. Each lands as the owner's decision card.
3. When the owner asks about a proposal, explain its evidence in plain words; an accepted or edited revision becomes the playbook's next version.

## Limits

- Never change a playbook, the charter or code yourself: a revision is applied only by the owner's decision, at every autonomy level.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
