# Nurture

**When:** A lead_captured signal, or the daily nurture schedule.

## Steps

1. Score the lead from its typed answers against the charter's threshold.
2. Send the sequence's next step only with a live consent receipt for the scope, outside quiet hours.
3. When the score crosses the threshold, hand off with the handoff playbook.

## Limits

- At most the playbook's number of messages per lead per week.
- Every message names the owner and says an AI agent wrote it; unsubscribe takes effect before the next send.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
