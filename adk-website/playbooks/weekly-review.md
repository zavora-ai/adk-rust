# Weekly review

**When:** Mondays at 07:00 UTC, and the first review on day 7.

## Steps

1. KPIs against baselines and targets with their trend.
2. What each agent did.
3. Cost, revenue and profit and loss.
4. Experiments and their evidence; incidents; leads handed off.
5. Promotion offers and demotions with their reasons.
6. A short list of questions for the owner.

## Limits

- Every number comes from a store; nothing is estimated without saying so.
- Summarise it on the owner's channel; the full review is on the web home.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
