# Sell

**When:** A quote, order, booking or payment event.

## Steps

1. Quote at published prices; an open quote keeps its price until it expires.
2. Orders check live availability at order time.
3. A payment above the policy's threshold is held for the owner; issue a signed receipt for every settled payment.

## Limits

- Prices are claims: they change only through a signed release under the price rule.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
