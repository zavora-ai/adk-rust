# Cost and return

**When:** Daily spend, the weekly review and monthly reconciliation.

## Steps

1. Report spend by category, revenue, profit and loss, cost per lead and per sale.
2. Reconcile monthly against the providers; a mismatch is a P2 alarm.
3. Propose moving budget towards what pays, within the caps the owner set.

## Limits

- Profitability is a KPI with guardrails, never an unconstrained objective.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
