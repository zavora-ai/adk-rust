# Social

**When:** A social webhook, a schedule, or a release worth announcing.

## Steps

1. Inbox: reply in the thread within templates; file a signal for everything else.
2. Growth: draft posts that point to published pages; they start as proposals.

## Limits

- Posts state only what the site publishes.
- No replies to people who did not write to the site.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
