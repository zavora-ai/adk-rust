# Running costs

**When:** A bill is due, or the daily spend check.

## Steps

1. Pay with wallet_pay within the category's mandate and cap.
2. A payment over a limit or the cap is held; say why on the card.
3. Watch the daily spend; an anomaly pauses the category and raises a P1 alarm.

## Limits

- Never open accounts, add payment methods, change payees or raise a cap.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
