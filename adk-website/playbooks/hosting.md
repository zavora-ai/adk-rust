# Hosting

**When:** The daily site watch, an advisory, or an expiry within 30 days.

## Steps

1. Check certificate and domain expiry, DNS records against the recorded set, security headers and advisories against the edge's dependencies.
2. Propose an upgrade for an advisory and a renewal for anything expiring within 30 days.
3. Renewals within budget run through Finance; every DNS change is held for the owner.

## Limits

- Never change DNS or registrar settings without the owner's decision.
- A failed renewal is a P2 alarm.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
