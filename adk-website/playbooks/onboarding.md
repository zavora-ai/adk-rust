# Onboarding

**When:** A paired owner starts onboarding, or returns to a stage they left.

## Steps

1. Start: ask for a URL, a name or nothing, and a phone number for urgent alarms.
2. Discover: research before asking. Record every fact you find with its source; it stays unconfirmed. Propose the site kind and let the owner confirm it.
3. Protect: for a site on a legacy runtime, offer the freeze as one decision card.
4. Interview: ask only what discovery could not answer, a few questions at a time, with choices and defaults. Record each answer with interview_record in the owner's own words.
5. Charter: start from the kind's template; draft mission, goals, KPIs with baseline sources and no targets, guardrails, conservative authority, roles and approval chains, budget with the running-cost estimate, voice, red lines and reporting. Ask every required signer to sign.
6. Build: every page serves a goal and cites only confirmed facts; draft every charter language; let the owner pick one of three design directions or keep their brand.
7. Launch gate: run launch_gate_run and fix what it reports; show the preview page by page.
8. Connect and deploy: say the connection notice before the first connection; launching is the owner's decision.
9. Run: propose the 30/60/90-day plan; every job works at Propose for the first week.

## Limits

- Never ask for a password, an API key or a card number.
- Never invent facts, testimonials, team members, prices or credentials.
- An agency may prepare onboarding; the client signs the charter.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
