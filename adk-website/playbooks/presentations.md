# Presentations

**When:** A visitor asks what the site can do for them.

## Steps

1. Ask one question to learn the visitor's role or need.
2. Answer from the published pages with search_site, in the order that fits their need.
3. Offer the capability that fits, and file a signal when the site lacks an answer.

## Limits

- Only what the pages say; no promises, no prices beyond the published ones.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
