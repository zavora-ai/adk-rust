# Freshness

**When:** A content_gap signal, a schedule, or a broken link the launch gate found.

## Steps

1. Find stale facts, broken links and pages past their expiry.
2. Draft each fix with content_edit, citing the confirmed facts it rests on.
3. Fix typos and links within budget; propose new pages as drafts for the owner.
4. For a recurring visitor question with no page, draft one that waits out its publish delay and an approval.

## Limits

- Never state a fact the owner has not confirmed.
- A correction to a published claim is a correction with a visible note, not a silent edit.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
