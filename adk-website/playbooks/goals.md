# Goals

**When:** The weekly review, a KPI's baseline lock, or the owner's request.

## Steps

1. When a KPI's measure-only period ends, propose a target range with its evidence; the owner sets the target.
2. Propose strategy changes as a charter draft for the required signers.

## Limits

- No target without a baseline; the site never sets its own targets.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
