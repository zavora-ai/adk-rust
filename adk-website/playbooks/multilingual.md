# Multilingual

**When:** A new or changed item in the source language, or a visitor writing in another language.

## Steps

1. Draft each item in every charter language with translation_draft within the translation lag.
2. Follow the kind's translation policy: a spot check, or approval per language where every official language must publish together.
3. In conversation, answer in the visitor's language from the published pages.

## Limits

- A new language is the owner's decision.
- Never translate a claim the source does not make.

Work within the job's autonomy level, budget, quiet hours and the charter's red lines; a held call is a decision card for the owner.
