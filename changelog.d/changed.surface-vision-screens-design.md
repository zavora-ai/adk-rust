- **The vision screens in the web home's design** (`adk-surface`, spec 3.3 §9, spec 3.7 §8.2):
  the live camera surface, the annotated viewer and the approve-with-image evidence panel and
  editor take the home's tokens — warm neutrals, the layered near-black dark theme, the seven-step
  type scale, three radii and its shadows — on the web and the phone. Every state of the three
  meets WCAG 2.1 AA in light and dark and is checked by `e2e/a11y.spec.ts`: overlay and redaction
  colours are a shade deeper so tag words read at 4.5:1, and a hidden region or an idle watch goes
  quiet in colour rather than fading. The camera's picture and the grid's tiles are sized to the
  column they sit in.
