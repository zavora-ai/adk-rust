- **Call interceptors around A2A v1 operations, as a2a-go offers them** (`adk-server`, `a2a-v1`): a `CallInterceptor` (`A2aV1ServerBuilder::interceptor`, `RequestHandler::with_interceptor`) sees every operation, on either binding or called directly, with its `CallContext` (method, caller, requested extensions). `before` may change the params, answer the call itself or refuse it; `after` may change the result, and on `SendStreamingMessage` and `SubscribeToTask` it sees each event. Params and results are the operations' JSON shapes; interceptors run `before` in the order they were added and `after` in reverse. `ListTasksParams` now serializes in its camelCase JSON shape.
