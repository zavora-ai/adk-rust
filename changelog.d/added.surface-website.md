- **The website's views on the web home** (`adk-surface` feature `website`, Experimental,
  `adk-website` module `ui`, spec 3.5 §8.2): `WebsiteDesk` turns a prebuilt website's
  stores into views in plain words, and the web home shows them at `/ui/website` — the setup
  code that pairs the owner's device (A-I20); onboarding's nine stages with discovery's facts
  and their sources, the interview's live preview, the charter as cards signed by every
  required signer, the looks, the launch check and connections that never ask for a password;
  the site view with releases and their page changes, KPIs, jobs to promote or lower, held
  updates, alarms, signals and the wallet; the weekly review and its summary sent to the
  owner's channel; and the web editor, a form per content kind with Markdown, images,
  scheduling, approvals, corrections and withdrawals. `web::website::router` and
  `setup_router` serve the desk under `/api/surface/website`; the `scripted_website` example
  serves a bakery's site fresh or a week after launch.
