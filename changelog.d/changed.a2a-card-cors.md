- **Any page may read the A2A agent card** (`adk-server`): as a2a-go serves it, `/.well-known/agent-card.json` and `/.well-known/agent.json` answer a CORS preflight (`GET`, `OPTIONS` with `Content-Type`, for a day) and echo the requesting page's `Origin` with credentials, or `*` without one. The origin guard no longer refuses cross-origin reads of the card; the host guard still refuses a rebinding `Host`, and every other route keeps `SecurityConfig::allowed_origins`.
