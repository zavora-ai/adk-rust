- **A fixed app shell, and answered cards that collapse to one line** (`adk-surface`): the web
  home, the tray panel and the phone fill their window and never scroll or bounce as a page;
  the header stays put and the roster, the thread, the right panel, Today and Memory & rules
  each scroll inside themselves, with the message box in view. A decided card, answered on
  any surface, collapses to one line — a check, its heading and who answered — that a click,
  Enter or Space opens to show the exact call again, never answering anything. On the tray the
  lines leave five minutes after the answer or when a new decision arrives, three at most.
  `DecidedLine` is the shared component (spec 3.3 §5.1, §5.4, §5.5, §7.1, §7.4).
