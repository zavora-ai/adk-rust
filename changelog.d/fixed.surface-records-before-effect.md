- **Answers, refusals and pairing are on record with their surface** (`adk-surface`,
  `adk-kernel`; spec 3.3 §11, S-I18): the engine records each answer as `surface.answer`
  before it takes effect and gives no answer it cannot record; a device's decision, a
  pairing code's issue and its claim name the surface and how; an agent change from the web
  home, the phone or the tray records its HTTP transport; a by-hand change applied with
  another digest is recorded as `kernel.agent.refused`; and a removal the kernel's tick
  finishes names the tick.
